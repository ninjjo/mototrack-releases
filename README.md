## Overview

**MotoTrack** is a high-precision, offline-first GPS telemetry and riding companion engineered for smooth 120Hz performance on Android. Whether you're carving canyon roads, commuting across the city, or riding with a crew, MotoTrack tracks every kilometer with studio-grade visuals and strict local-first privacy.

---

## Key Features

### Live Telemetry & Navigation
- **Distraction-Free Riding Focus Mode:** Automatically hides idle clutter while recording, keeping only Speed, Time, Distance, and 1-tap Emergency SOS visible.
- **Google Maps-Style Active Navigation:** Route preview (`Start Navigation` or `Start + Record`), look-ahead zoomed camera, heading-up rotation, navigation arrow marker, turn-by-turn top banner, ETA bar, and subtle haptic turn cues.
- **4 Adaptive Map Layers:** Switch anytime between **Navigation**, **Streets**, **Real Terrain (Outdoors)**, and **Satellite Hybrid** — all adapting smoothly to Dark and Light modes.
- **Snap-to-Road GPS Matching:** Aligns recorded routes cleanly onto real street geometry with color-coded **Speed Heatmap** overlays.
- **Acceleration Benchmarks:** Automatic `0–60 km/h` and `0–100 km/h` run detection with Personal Records trophy tracking.
- **Dockable Speedometer & Custom Themes:** Long-press to collapse the central dial into a compact pill, choose from 3 Speedometer Dash styles, Dark/Light mode, 7 preset accents, or craft any custom color with the interactive **HSV Color Wheel**.

### Garage, Maintenance & Fuel Analytics
- **Multi-Vehicle Garage:** Manage motorcycles and cars with odometer tracking and total vehicle spend (Fuel + Maintenance).
- **Smart Category-Tailored Maintenance Log:** Dedicated forms for Oil Changes (viscosity, filter toggle, quick `+1,000 / +3,000 / +5,000 km` presets), Licenses & Insurance (date-only expiry tracking), Brake Pads & Tires (`Front / Rear / Both`), Batteries, and more.
- **Dedicated Reminders Center:** Schedule real Android phone notifications by exact Date & Time or Odometer mileage.
- **Fuel Efficiency Analytics:** Log fill-ups with automatic price/liter calculation and an interactive `km/L` trend chart.

### Safety, Privacy & Offline Reliability
- **Emergency SOS:** Save up to 3 emergency contacts (with native Android Contact Picker), 1-tap **Call Now**, **WhatsApp GPS Pin**, **SMS Pin**, plus quick-dial **Police (122)** & **Ambulance (123)**.
- **Privacy / Ghost Zones:** Automatically trims routes and places opaque visual shield circles over your hidden areas on exported files and Share Ride Cards.
- **Offline Maps Downloader:** Pre-download city and highway map regions (up to 250 MB) directly to device storage.
- **Full Backup & Export:** Export rides as `GPX`, `KML`, `CSV`, `JSON`, or crisp 2x HD **Share Ride Cards** (9:16 Story & 1:1 Square), plus full `.json` app backup & restore.

### Social & Riding Crews
- **Riding Buddies & Groups (2–10 Riders):** Connect via unique Rider IDs (`MOTO-XXXX`), create & manage riding crews, see real-time **`On a Ride`** status indicators, and view friends' profiles.
- **Automatic In-App Updates:** Instant notification inside MotoTrack whenever a new release drops.

## Privacy & Security

- **100% Local-First Telemetry:** Your GPS tracks, ride history, ride photos, garage vehicles, fuel logs, maintenance records, and SOS contacts stay stored locally on your device.
- **Transparent Cloud Sync:** Optional Firebase authentication & Firestore sync are used strictly for your public Rider ID card, Friend Requests, Riding Groups, and live `"On a Ride"` status — and can be erased in 1 tap via **Settings → Delete Cloud Profile & Social Data**.
